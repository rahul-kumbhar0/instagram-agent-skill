# ig-channel

A broadcast channel is the one surface where Instagram still sends a
notification: a one-to-many channel in the DM tab that subscribers opted
into. Story reach decays; channel delivery doesn't. That makes it the
highest-trust, lowest-volume surface the account has - and the fastest one to
burn by over-posting.

## Cadence is the strategy

- **1-2 posts a day, max.** Past 3, unsubscribes spike (vendor consensus, not
  Meta data - but the mechanism is obvious: every post pings a phone).
- Skip days beat filler. A channel that only speaks when it has something is
  a channel people stay in.
- Fixed rituals work: a Monday "this week I'm making X", a Friday poll. The
  ig-strategist agent should place channel beats in the weekly plan.

## What a channel post is

Short, lowercase-energy, person-to-person - closer to a text message than a
caption. The humanizer gate applies double here; one stock phrase and it
reads like a newsletter. Four post types, rotate them:

1. **The early look** - the reel before it posts, the cover vote ("A or B?"),
   the half-finished thing. Channels exist to make subscribers insiders.
2. **The poll** - one question, real stakes ("which of these two should I
   actually make?"). Poll results are free research; feed them back to the
   ig-research agent.
3. **The reply prompt** - a question that moves people into real DMs, where
   the `/ig-dm` rules take over. The channel is top of the funnel, not the
   funnel.
4. **The drop** - the launch, the link, the announcement. Earn it with the
   other three; a channel that only sells empties out.

## Growing the channel

- The **"join channel" story sticker** is the main driver - pair a story
  sequence (`/ig-story`) with a reason to join *today* (the early look
  works).
- Mention the channel in reel CTAs sparingly - once per week, not per reel.
- Seed it in the keyword DM: after delivering what the comment asked for, one
  line: "I share this stuff early in my channel" (coordinate with the
  ig-engager agent's automation copy).

## Rules

- Channel posts are manual sends by the user - no API publishes to channels.
  Everything here ends as copy-ready blocks with a suggested send time.
- Don't start a channel for an account under ~1k engaged followers; say so.
  An empty channel reads worse than no channel.
- Track one number monthly: subscriber count vs. average story views. When
  the channel passes stories, it becomes the primary announcement surface.
